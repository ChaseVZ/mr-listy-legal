Privacy Policy

App: Mr. Listy
Effective date: September 19, 2026
Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mr. Listy (“the App,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Mr. Listy is a collaborative list app for households and small groups (grocery lists, watchlists, shared expenses, and gift / wishlist ideas).

Contact: mr_listy@yahoo.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, we receive identifiers and profile details from that provider (such as a user ID, name, and email address, if shared). Apple may allow you to hide your email.
  • Profile data you provide. Display name, optional profile photo, friend code, and gift-list tier labels you configure.
  • User-generated content. List items and related data you create or share, including grocery and watchlist entries, bill / expense information, gift wishlist items (title, optional price, description, image, claim/purchase status), and group membership.
  • Friends and invites. Friend codes, friend requests, and friendships between accounts.
  • Device preferences. Settings stored on your device only (for example theme and timezone preferences), via local storage on the device.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and error reports via Firebase Crashlytics (device/app information related to failures).

We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, or microphone/camera capture for this App. Photos are chosen from your photo library when you opt to upload a profile or gift image.

3. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and sync your lists
  • To let you collaborate with friends and group members you choose
  • To show shared list and expense history to other members of a group
  • To improve reliability and fix bugs (Crashlytics)
  • To respond to support requests you send us

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How information is shared

  • With people you collaborate with. Friends and group members can see relevant profile details (such as display name and photo) and shared list content for groups you join.
  • Service providers. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Crashlytics) to operate the App. Those services process data on our behalf under their terms and privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the App.

When you leave a group or delete your account, shared group history may retain a display-name label so remaining members can still understand past list or expense entries. That is not a full account and cannot be used to sign in.

5. Data storage and security

Account and list data are stored in Google Firebase. We use access controls and industry-standard transport security (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Data retention and account deletion

We retain account and list data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the App (Profile → Delete account). Deletion removes your account, private list data, friends/invites, and profile photo, and removes you from groups. Shared group content may remain for other members, sometimes with your former display name on historical entries.

You may also contact us at the email above to request help with deletion.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Third-party services

Sign-in and backend services include:

  • Google Sign-In / Google Firebase
  • Sign in with Apple (on Apple platforms)

Their processing is also governed by their own privacy policies.

9. International users

If you use the App from outside the country where our providers operate, your information may be processed in other countries (for example via Firebase infrastructure).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
